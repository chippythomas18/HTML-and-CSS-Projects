# 🎉 My Portfolio Website

✅ **Mobile Friendly** - Looks good on phones, tablets, and computers
✅ **Smooth Navigation** - Click navbar links to jump to sections

✅ **Contact Form** - People can email you directly from your site
✅ **Projects Showcase** - Display all your coding projects
